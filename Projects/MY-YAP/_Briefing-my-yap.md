## Overview

Short-form or long-form video content channel where Nigel shares opinionated takes on education, real-world skills, and lifelong learning. The core thesis: the traditional education system is outdated and teaches the wrong things — resilience, shamelessness, and relentless pursuit matter more than test scores and memorisation.

Target audience: young professionals and parents questioning conventional education paths. The personal angle: Nigel has a 3.5-year-old and is actively thinking about what education should look like.

## Key Themes

- **School is outdated:** test-based and essay-based learning is too slow, produces average outcomes, no longer signals a productive citizen
- **Alternative education:** schoolai.com, alpha.school as examples of evolution
- **Real-world skills:** not taking no for an answer, being shameless, relentless attitude to finding success "just once"
- **Continuous learning:** diplomas are a starting point, not an endpoint
