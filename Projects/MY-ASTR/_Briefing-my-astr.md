## Overview

Celesto is an astrology/timing intelligence app. Two positioning tracks explored:
1. **Consumer astrology app** — horoscopes, birth charts, daily insights
2. **Professional timing/productivity tool** — auspicious timing, "kairos" framing for executives

Currently parked in validation — not abandoned.

---

## Traction

- Landing page live (single-page HTML)
- ~300 email signups captured
- Meta ads infrastructure live (using "Deserve" FB page as backend)
- Scoring quiz built as conversion mechanism

---

## GTM Learnings

- **Quiz is conversion, not traffic.** The scorecard quiz generates purchase intent; it's not the acquisition channel
- **Pricing placement matters.** CTA on result screen generates real intent vs. early CTAs that get ignored
- **Ad creative insight:** Native UI mimicry (iMessage-style creatives) + emotional pain hooks + specificity for believability — technique from AstroTalk high-performers
- **Email tools evaluated:** Brevo, Buttondown, GMass

---
