Like who can do that?

And drastic times calls for drastic measures. 

I need something on my side. Something that I can lean on to help guide me through all this uncertainty. When it's a really bad day, it makes a difference to know if it wasn't my fault and it was just meant to be rough. 

Enter woo-woo. Or as I'd like to call it, the mystic arts.

But that's also why I'm making this channel. 

Can I convince myself that woo-woo can be a yes-yes? If I learn enough of these different traditions, will I find a pattern that makes sense to me? Can I use AI for this

And ultimately, can I convince others like me to get into it as well? 

I going to deep dive into whatever I can get my hands on - astrology, tarot, chinese zodiacs, etc. And I'll document my learnings and opinions of what I find. (Maybe I should do a slide deck...)
