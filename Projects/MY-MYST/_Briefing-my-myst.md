# MY-MYST — Mystic Scientist (Content Channel)

> **Status:** Active — pre-launch (scripts drafted, no content published yet)
> **Type:** Owned product (content/media)
> **Started:** ~May 2026
> **Platform:** YouTube / social media

---

## Overview

Content channel where Nigel explores mystical traditions (astrology, tarot, numerology, Chinese zodiacs, palmistry) from a skeptic/consultant perspective. Brand positioning: "From Consultant to Mystic Scientist." The hook is the unlikely profile — ex-consultant, accounting major, tech commercial director — approaching woo-woo with analytical rigour and AI tools.

Target audience: professionals who feel the "ick" about mystical content but consume it anyway. People who are privately curious but publicly skeptical.

---

## Content Strategy

**Content pillars:**
1. **Personal journey** — vulnerability-driven narrative about uncertainty, career pressure, seeking guidance
2. **AI meets mysticism** — using frontier AI models to cross-validate mystical predictions (the "AI Mystic Matrix")
3. **Deep dives** — research logs into individual traditions (astrology, tarot, numerology, etc.)
4. **Fix & iterate** — transparent improvement of the AI methodology based on deeper domain knowledge

**Format:** Talking head, studio setting, simple visuals. Research log series planned.

**The AI Mystic Matrix concept:** An app combining multiple mystical traditions (astrology, tarot, numerology, horoscopes) × multiple AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen) to independently generate predictions, then an AI judge identifies consistent patterns across all of them. The thesis: if 5 traditions × 5 models converge on something, it's harder to dismiss.

## Key Decisions

- **Skeptic persona is the brand differentiator.** Not another astrology channel — a consultant learning mysticism out loud.
- **AI is the analytical layer.** Position AI as the tool that makes mysticism credible to skeptics.
- **Multi-tradition, multi-model approach** avoids anchoring to any single system.
